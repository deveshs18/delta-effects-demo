Payroll Processing

Paydays should be predictable. We run payroll, handle deposits and filings, and keep your team paid on time so you can focus on the work.

Overview

Delta Effects handles the payroll cycle from pay run through deposits, quarterly filings, and W-2s. You get a dedicated process, not a seasonal add-on—so employees, contractors, and tax accounts stay in sync.

Who it's for

Who this service is for

Small businesses that want payroll done correctly without building an HR department.

  • 01Owners tired of running payroll at night and hoping deposits posted
  • 02Teams adding employees and needing a reliable cadence
  • 03Businesses that want payroll, books, and tax in one story
  • 04Employers who need W-2s and filings on time every year
